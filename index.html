🗣️ Interactive Dialogues

Learn how to ask and answer about school & home time!

Dialogue 1 Topic: School Time
A
你几点去上学？

nǐ jǐ diǎn qù shàng xué?

What time do you go to school?

B
我七点去上学。

wǒ qī diǎn qù shàng xué.

I go to school at 7 o'clock. 🕒 7:00

Dialogue 2 Topic: Home Time
A
你几点回家？

nǐ jǐ diǎn huí jiā?

What time do you go home?

B
我两点回家。

wǒ liǎng diǎn huí jiā.

I go home at 2 o'clock. 🕑 2:00
